Linear Features & Use Cases
Linear is designed for fast, software-centric workflows. Its minimalist approach helps engineering and product teams stay in the zone—without distractions.
🌟 Highlights
- 🌀 Sprint planning – Built-in tools for agile project cycles
- 🛠️ Linear issue tracking – Perfect for devs using GitHub or GitLab
- ⌨️ Keyboard shortcuts – Work faster without the mouse
- 🧼 Clean UX – Reduces distraction and cognitive load
👥 Ideal for
- 🚀 Fast-moving software teams
- 🧩 Agile sprints and issue resolution
- 💻 Developers who prefer keyboard-based UX

Notion Features & Use Cases
Notion combines docs, databases, wikis, and task management—all in one flexible workspace.
🌟 Highlights
- 📝 Documentation – Store meeting notes, SOPs, and project plans
- 📊 Custom databases – Track anything from clients to bugs
- 🗂️ Kanban, table, list views – Choose the layout that fits your workflow
- 🔗 Notion integrations – Automate and centralize your processes, don’t forget about the aesthetic Notion templates
👥 Ideal for
- 🤝 Cross-functional teams
- 🎨 Marketing, HR, and creative planning
- 📚 Company wikis and knowledge management
✅ Everhour brings real-time time tracking to Notion via its Notion time tracking integration. Log time by task or project, visualize workloads, and automate reports—all from inside Notion.
