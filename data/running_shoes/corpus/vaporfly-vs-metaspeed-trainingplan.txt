Nike Vaporfly vs ASICS Metaspeed Sky: Which Running Shoe Is Right for You?
Compare the Nike Vaporfly 3 and ASICS Metaspeed Sky Paris, two elite carbon racing shoes. Find which super shoe will help you run your fastest times.

The Nike Vaporfly and ASICS Metaspeed Sky represent the pinnacle of racing shoe technology. Both feature carbon plates, super foams, and designs optimized for breaking personal records. Choosing between them comes down to fit, feel, and running style preferences.
Quick Verdict
Choose the Nike Vaporfly 3 if you want the proven super shoe that started the revolution. It's lighter at shorter distances, more widely tested, and slightly cheaper. It works for virtually all running styles and race distances.
Choose the ASICS Metaspeed Sky Paris if you prefer a bit more cushioning, identify as a stride runner, or like ASICS' fit. It offers comparable performance with a slightly different ride character.

Head-to-Head Comparison
Performance
Both shoes deliver exceptional racing performance—you won't go wrong with either. The Vaporfly feels snappier and more immediately responsive, which many runners prefer for shorter distances. The Metaspeed Sky's higher stack and additional cushioning can feel better over marathon distances.
Studies show both improve running economy by similar margins. The difference is feel, not measurable performance.
Winner: Tie — Both are elite racing tools.
Weight
The Vaporfly weighs 6.6 oz for men and 5.6 oz for women. The Metaspeed Sky comes in at 6.5 oz for men and 5.5 oz for women. These are essentially identical—differences too small to notice.
Winner: Tie — Negligible difference.
Cushioning
The Metaspeed Sky has more foam—44mm at the heel versus 40mm for the Vaporfly. This translates to slightly more cushioning and impact protection, particularly noticeable in the later miles of a marathon.
The Vaporfly's ZoomX foam is softer and bouncier, while the Metaspeed Sky's FF TURBO PLUS feels firmer but returns energy efficiently. Both protect well; they just do it differently.
Winner: ASICS Metaspeed Sky — Higher stack, more cushioning.
Responsiveness
The Vaporfly has a snappier feel that many runners prefer for quick turnover. Its lower stack height creates a more connected-to-ground sensation while still providing the carbon plate propulsion.
The Metaspeed Sky's higher stack adds a millisecond of delay in energy return, which stride runners often prefer—it matches their longer ground contact time. Cadence runners may find it slightly less responsive.
Winner: Nike Vaporfly — Snappier response for most running styles.
Price/Value
The Vaporfly costs MSRP: $260; the Metaspeed Sky costs MSRP: $275. Both are expensive and neither is a good "value" in traditional terms—these are specialized tools for competitive racing.
The Vaporfly's lower price and broader suitability make it the marginally better value for most runners.
Winner: Nike Vaporfly — MSRP: $15 cheaper, equally effective.
Fit
The Vaporfly has a narrower, more precise fit that some love and others find restrictive. It only comes in standard width.
The Metaspeed Sky also only offers standard width but tends to feel slightly more accommodating. Neither shoe is ideal for wide feet.
Winner: ASICS Metaspeed Sky — Slightly more accommodating fit.

Final Verdict
The Nike Vaporfly and ASICS Metaspeed Sky are both exceptional racing shoes that will help you run faster. The performance difference between them is minimal—both deliver the 3-4% economy improvement that defines super shoes.
The Vaporfly is the better choice for most runners because it's proven, versatile, and works for all running styles. It's been tested in more world records and major marathons than any other shoe. If you're investing in a super shoe and don't have strong preferences, the Vaporfly is the safe bet.
The Metaspeed Sky is the better choice for stride runners who generate speed through push-off rather than quick turnover. It's also worth considering if you've found the Vaporfly uncomfortable or want slightly more cushioning for marathon distances.
Both shoes will help you race faster. The best choice is the one that fits your foot and running style—and the only way to know for sure is to try them. If you can only pick one, the Vaporfly's broader applicability makes it the default recommendation.
